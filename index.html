Theoretical Foundations & Pedagogical Co-Pilots

  • Ecological Systems Theory (Bronfenbrenner, 1979): Aligns school, peer, and classroom environments around the learner.
  • Sociocultural Theory & ZPD (Vygotsky, 1978): Structured peer scaffolding through trained Academic Buddies.
  • Differentiated Instruction (Tomlinson, 2017): Tailors lesson formats to student readiness and learning profiles.

16-Week Pilot Implementation Roadmap

Weeks 1–3 School coordination, initial literacy-numeracy mapping, local Wi-Fi setup, and Academic Buddy training.
Weeks 4–12 Classroom module integration, twice-weekly peer mentoring sessions, and periodic progress monitoring.
Weeks 13–16 Outcome evaluation (literacy, numeracy, participation, SUS usability) and Teacher Champion handover.
